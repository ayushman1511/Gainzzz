# PRD — Anime Fitness Evolution App

## 1. Product Overview

### Product Name
Anime Fitness Evolution (working title)

### Product Vision
Create a gamified fitness mobile application that transforms real-world fitness progress into an anime-inspired progression system, helping users stay consistent, motivated, and emotionally connected to their fitness journey.

### Problem Statement
Most fitness apps fail because:
- workouts feel repetitive and boring
- users lack emotional motivation
- there is no strong identity or progression system
- beginners feel overwhelmed

This app solves the problem by turning fitness into a progression-based experience inspired by anime growth arcs, leveling systems, and character evolution.

---

# 2. Core Concept

The app starts by onboarding users through questions about:
- body type
- fitness level
- stamina
- goals
- experience
- consistency

Based on the answers, the system assigns the user an anime-style character archetype representing their current fitness stage.

Examples:
- Rookie Fighter
- Shadow Runner
- Iron Monk
- Beast Tank
- Flame Striker

As users complete real-world fitness tasks:
- running
- pushups
- squats
- hydration
- sleep goals
- step goals

they earn:
- XP
- levels
- achievements
- evolution stages

Their anime character visually evolves over time.
